INTERPRETATION GUIDE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * At beta=0.50 the gate retains ~99% of blocks, so a near-zero dMAE there
    is expected by construction and is NOT evidence the gate is useless.
  * The meaningful signal is the TREND across beta: if MAE falls as beta
    rises, the gate's ranking is informative even when the operating point
    is permissive.
  * If MAE is flat across the whole sweep, the honest conclusion is that the
    gate does not measurably improve this downstream estimator on this
    cohort -- report it plainly rather than leaving the claim implicit.
