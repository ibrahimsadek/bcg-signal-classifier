     should say half-rate is ONE contributor, not THE mechanism.

  NOTE: this is a block-level HR-ratio test, which is the practical proxy for
  the interval-ratio histogram the manuscript proposes. It shares the
  hypothesis's logic but aggregates within 30-s blocks, so a block mixing
  correct and halved beats lands between the modes rather than at 0.5.
