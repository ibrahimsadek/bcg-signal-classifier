NOTE ON JJ-COUNT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Keeping blocks with the MOST detected J-J intervals is WORSE than
  random at 66% coverage (14.759 vs 13.909). More detectable
  peaks indicates artefact, not signal -- the same inversion already
  documented for RMS energy in the internal feature baselines.
  Regularity of beat spacing predicts usability; quantity of peaks
  actively misleads. That is a coherent physiological thread running
  through both halves of the paper and is worth stating explicitly.
