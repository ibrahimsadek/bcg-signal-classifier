INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Decision agreement is EXACTLY 100%.

  This is the expected result, not a coincidence. Temperature scaling
  divides logits by a single T > 0 before the softmax. For binary
  classification at a fixed threshold this is strictly monotonic, so it
  cannot move any window across the decision boundary. The calibrated and
  uncalibrated gates emit the same accept/reject mask, therefore identical
  block coverage and identical downstream HR error -- there is no MAE,
  coverage or F1 difference to report, by construction.

  Calibration changes CONFIDENCE (Brier / ECE / NLL), not DECISIONS. The
  manuscript reports those internal calibration effects already, and they
  were negligible on average (dECE = +0.0026).

  SUGGESTED REBUTTAL WORDING:
    'We verified this directly on the external cohort: calibrated and
     uncalibrated gates agreed on 100% of N 1-second windows, because
     temperature scaling is a monotonic transform of the logits and so
     cannot alter the argmax at a fixed threshold. Downstream coverage and
     HR error are consequently identical. We have added a sentence to the
     external-validation section making this explicit.'
