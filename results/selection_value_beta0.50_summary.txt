VERDICT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  => THE GATE'S SELECTION CARRIES NO DEMONSTRABLE INFORMATION.

     Dropping the same number of blocks AT RANDOM does as well or better.
     The MAE improvement seen in patch6 is therefore a sample-shrinkage
     artefact, not evidence of discrimination: any procedure that discards
     a third of blocks lowers the MAE of what remains.

     This is the single most important negative result in the study and it
     must be reported. It does NOT invalidate the classifier's internal
     metrics -- it shows those metrics do not translate into downstream
     selection value on this cohort and this estimator.

  SCOPE: this tests selection quality at ONE coverage level, on ONE
  downstream estimator. It says nothing about the classifier's agreement
  with the human annotator, which is a separate question the internal
  evaluation already answers.
