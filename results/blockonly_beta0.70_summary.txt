VERDICT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  => INTERVAL-LEVEL RESTRICTION IS NEUTRAL.

     On common blocks the two arms are effectively identical, so the
     total effect (-0.239 bpm) is essentially all SELECTION: the
     arms differ because they score different block sets, not because
     gating changes the estimate on a given block.

     This means the gate is REJECTING BLOCKS THAT WOULD HAVE BEEN
     ESTIMATED WELL -- it is discarding usable signal. Check the error
     distribution of the blocks in 'scored only in ungated'.

  NOTE ON beta=0.90: its apparently low MAE in patch1 is NOT gate value.
  At 3.3% coverage the arms score entirely different block subsets, so that
  comparison is selection, not performance. Only the common-block numbers
  above support a like-for-like claim.
